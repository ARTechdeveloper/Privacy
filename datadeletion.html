8. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

No Account Requirement

Our App does not require user registration or login. Therefore, we do not collect, link, or store personal identifiers such as your email address, name, or phone number on our servers.

Data Retention

Anonymous technical data and feedback communications are retained only as long as necessary for operational support, troubleshooting, and legal compliance.

How to Delete Your Data & Deletion Rights

  • Local Data (Images & Icons): All images, custom icons, and preferences are processed and saved locally on your device. You can completely delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data/cache in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • Anonymous Technical & Ad Data (If Applicable): For technical data (like crash logs) and Advertising IDs collected by third-party services (such as Google AdMob or Firebase, if active), there is no personal account linked to this data, making it impossible for us to manually identify and delete a specific user's logs. However, you can reset your Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads directly from your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID).
  • Account & Server Data Deletion: If user account creation or cloud database features are enabled in future versions, users will have access to an in-app deletion option as well as a publicly accessible web link to request the deletion of their account and associated data. Upon receiving a valid request, server data will be permanently erased within 30 to 90 days, except where retention is required by applicable law.
